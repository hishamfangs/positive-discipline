About Nagwa El Saadani

Nagwa is a certified Positive Discipline educator committed to helping parents nurture confident, cooperative children through playful learning and compassionate leadership.

  • Certified Positive Discipline Parent Educator
  • Serving families across Dubai & Cairo
  • Workshops tailored for toddlers to teens

I’m an expat mom of three and the founder of Positive Discipline DXB. As a certified Positive Discipline Parent and Classroom Educator, and a proud member of the American Association for Positive Discipline, I’ve experienced firsthand the magic this approach brings—both at home and in my workshops.
My mission is to help families discover that true change happens when we motivate through connection, not control. With the right tools and mindset, you can transform your family dynamics and parent with calm, confidence, and heart.
